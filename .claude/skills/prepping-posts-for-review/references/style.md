# Captions and hashtags

Defaults that work. The owner's own rules live in `data/post-style.md` (per-install and
gitignored) — read it first if it exists, and offer to write one from their answers the first
time you prep a batch. Their rules win over anything here.

## Hashtags

**Real tags people actually post to — not descriptions of the photo.** `#JacketOn` and
`#ButtonedUp` describe what is in the image and nobody searches or posts them. `#GroomsTux`
and `#BlackTie` are tags with a real audience.

Aim between the two failure modes: not so broad the post drowns (`#love`, `#photography`),
not so specific that the tag gets a handful of posts a year. Not cheesy.

**The first hashtag is the Threads topic**, so it must be the one most specific to what kind
of photo this is — `#CakeCutting` on the cake-cutting post, with the broader ones after it.

Reuse across a themed batch is fine and expected. The guard is that no two posts carry an
*identical* set, which the builder enforces, and that one tag isn't doing all the work — the
page lists every tag used more than once so you can see.

Three to five per post is plenty.

## Captions

**Each caption stands on its own.** Evergreen posts are shuffled by autofill and may be
scheduled in any order, so nothing may lean on a neighbour: no "a few more from that night",
no "and then…", no "swipe for part two" unless the swipe is within that same carousel.

Write what the moment was, not what the photo shows. "Cocktails before dinner at the villa"
beats "a photo of two drinks".

Say something concrete when you know it and ask when you don't — venue names, dishes, places
and people are what make these worth posting, and a wrong guess is worse than a question.

Check what the account has already posted before writing anything that contradicts it.

## Credit

If the photos came from a photographer, set `"credit"` in the plan once (for example
`📸 @studio`) and it is appended to every caption in the batch. Ask whether credit is wanted
rather than assuming — it differs per batch.

## A per-install style file

Offer to write `data/post-style.md` the first time, covering:

- photographer credit, if any, and how they want it written
- accounts and people they tag, and who must never be named
- words, emoji or claims to avoid
- hashtags they consider theirs, and ones they dislike
- how much they want captions to sound like them versus plain
